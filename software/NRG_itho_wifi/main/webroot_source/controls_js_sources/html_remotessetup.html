RF devices setup


A range of RF devices (e.g. remotes, CO2 and RV sensors) can be joined to the add-on using the CC1101 RF module.
The add-on will translate commands from a RF device to speed/timer commands towards the Itho.
This way the speed status of a RF device is always correctly represented on the add-on and within your domotica software.

There is also an option to only monitor RF devices. With this option checked, a RF device still paired with an Itho unit can be monitored without influencing the commands to your Itho.

Changing the remote function to "Send" will enable the user to send RF commands. The add-on can present itself with this function as a remote to your Itho.

Last received commands (and if applicable data) received from paired RF devices is available through the MQTT API and WebAPI

Itho Spider thermostat:

The add-on can read and set the heating setpoint of an Itho Spider room thermostat, without pairing it. Start discovery and set the Spider to the temperature shown (a random value between 21.5 and 24 °C). The add-on recognises the Spider by that value, finds the heater it controls and adds the Spider as a monitor-only RF device. Your heating may switch on during discovery. After that the setpoint can be changed here, via MQTT/REST or from Home Assistant.

  

Experimental: bind the add-on as the Spider's gateway, the way an Itho gateway or the RAMSES blueprint does. Press 'Bind as gateway' and put the Spider in pairing mode within 3 minutes (see the Spider manual). The Spider then asks the add-on for its setpoint (about every 9 minutes and when you touch its display) and takes the new value itself, also on its display. Its link to the heater or AutoTemp should stay as it is. Only for Spiders that offer this in their pairing message; other thermostats are ignored.
