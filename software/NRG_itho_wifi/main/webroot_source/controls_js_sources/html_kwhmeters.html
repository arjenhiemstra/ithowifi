kWh meters

RF kWh meters (Itho/Klimaatgarant) are read over RF on request. Enter the id printed on the meter (six hex characters, e.g. 77FC4D), give it a name and save. The add-on then polls every meter once per interval and publishes the counters on the WebAPI (/api/v2/kwhmeters), MQTT (kwhmeter/<id>) and HA discovery.

kWh meter polling is switched off. Enable it on the RF settings page first.

Every meter is asked once per interval (10 to 3600 s).
